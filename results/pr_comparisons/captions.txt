Validation precision–recall curves on the same 1,000 TweetEval-hate examples. Rows show models; columns show seeds. Colors identify training strategies. Circles mark threshold 0.5; squares mark validation-tuned thresholds. The dashed line is hate-class prevalence (42.7%). Legends report average precision (AP) and selected epoch. The f1_at_05 figure uses checkpoints selected by validation macro-F1 at 0.5; the average_precision figure uses checkpoints selected by validation AP. Curves show individual seeds, not averaged curves. All selection and tuning use validation data; these figures are not held-out test estimates.
